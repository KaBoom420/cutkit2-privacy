Privacy Policy for CutKit 2

Last updated: April 18, 2026

CutKit 2 is an offline utility app for calculating and visualizing optimal rectangular layouts.

Data Collection

The app does not require user registration. However, it may collect limited data through third-party services such as Firebase Analytics and Google Ads. This data may include device information, usage data, and diagnostics, and is used to improve the app and provide relevant content.

Internet Access

An internet connection is not required for core functionality, but may be used for analytics and advertising services.

Generated Files

The app allows users to export generated results such as PDF and DXF files. These files are created locally on the device and are shared only when the user explicitly initiates such action.

Third-Party Services

CutKit 2 uses third-party services, including Firebase Analytics and Google Ads. These services may collect anonymized or pseudonymized data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Permissions

The app operates without requiring unnecessary device permissions. Export and file sharing are performed using standard system mechanisms.
