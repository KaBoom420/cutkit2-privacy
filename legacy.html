Product Information

CutKit 2 is a software tool designed for optimizing the layout of rectangular parts on material sheets. It helps users estimate material usage and prepare efficient layouts for cutting, manufacturing, printing, CNC workflows, PCB panelization, signage production, and similar real-world tasks.

The application provides calculation results and export options intended to support planning and production preparation.
